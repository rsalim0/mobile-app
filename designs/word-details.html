eloquent

/ˈɛləkwənt/
adjective
  1. 1

    Fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing.

    "an eloquent speech that moved the entire room."

  2. 2

    Clearly expressing or indicating something.

    "her eloquent eyes spoke of deep sorrow."

noun (archaic)
  1. 1

    A person of eloquence.

Synonyms

articulate expressive silver-tongued fluent
